Privacy Policy

This page describes the information this application currently handles. It is written from the code as it exists today and is not legal advice.

Last reviewed: September 7, 2026

Information We Handle

When you create an account, the application stores a username, a password hash, and at least one account identifier: an email address or phone number. Passwords are hashed before they are stored. The application does not intentionally store plain-text passwords.

The application also stores course and lesson data, including lesson titles, durations, video references, and uploaded video files managed by an administrator.

Why It Is Used

Account information is used to create accounts, authenticate users, check account availability, protect member pages, and identify the account used to access course content. Course data is used to display lessons and operate the learning experience.

Browser Storage and Cookies

The server uses a Flask session cookie to maintain signed-in sessions. It is configured as HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax. The watch pages also use browser localStorage to store completed lesson indexes separately for the main and backend courses. This is used for course progress on that browser.

No analytics, advertising pixels, marketing trackers, or non-essential cookies were found in this codebase.

Third-Party Services

The pages request fonts from Google Fonts. When a user starts a YouTube lesson, the page may load YouTube's IFrame API or a YouTube embed. Those services may receive the technical information normally sent by a browser when connecting to them. Review the providers' own policies for details.

Sharing and Retention

The application does not contain an analytics or advertising pipeline, and no deliberate data-sharing integration was found. Account records remain in the local users data file until an administrator removes them. A formal retention schedule, deletion workflow, business operator, and privacy contact are not configured in this project and require owner input.

Your Choices

You can avoid creating an account, sign out, and clear the course-progress localStorage entries in your browser. There is currently no self-service account deletion or data export feature. To request deletion or ask a privacy question, use the configured privacy contact once the site owner provides one.

Privacy and support contact: 09016815405. Owner configuration required: add an email address or dedicated privacy support route before publishing this page.

Legal Review
