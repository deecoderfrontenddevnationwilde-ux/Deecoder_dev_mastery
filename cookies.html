Cookie Policy

This policy describes the browser storage and connections currently visible in the application.

Last reviewed: September 7, 2026

Necessary Session Cookie

Flask uses a session cookie to keep a signed-in user or administrator authenticated. It is HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax. Without it, protected pages and APIs cannot maintain a session.

Course Progress Storage

The watch pages use localStorage keys for completed lesson indexes. This is browser-side functional storage, not an analytics identifier. It can be cleared through the browser's site-data controls.

Third-Party Connections

Google Fonts may be requested when pages load. YouTube may be requested when a YouTube lesson is started. These are third-party connections rather than application analytics cookies. No advertising or analytics scripts were found.

Consent and Controls

The current codebase does not load analytics or advertising technologies that would require a non-essential consent mechanism. A cookie banner has therefore not been added. If future analytics, advertising, or other non-essential storage is introduced, the owner should review consent requirements for the relevant jurisdictions before enabling it.
